Course Summary and Research Areas

Prof. Soumya Kanti Ghosh · Dept. of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Kharagpur

Final lecture of the first half — a recap of all topics covered in lectures 1–39 and a survey of active cloud computing research directions. No new technical content; useful only as a high-level course map or for identifying research areas. Skim if pressed for time.

Summary
Course philosophy: Cloud computing is not a single new technology — it is the convergence of utility computing, SOA, virtualization, and scalable infrastructure into an "everything as a service" paradigm.
Core open problems: Energy/green management, security and privacy at scale, real-time big data analytics, and seamless IoT/sensor integration remain the most active research areas.
Practical note: Cloud simulators (e.g., CloudSim) are available for experimentation without requiring actual cloud infrastructure — useful for research without access to commercial deployments.
